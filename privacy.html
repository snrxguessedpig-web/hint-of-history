Privacy Policy

Hint of History Analytics · Last updated September 30, 2026

Hint of History Analytics ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hint of History YouTube channel to review that channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app uses YouTube API Services with two read-only permissions:

  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): the channel's name and subscriber count, its uploaded videos (titles, descriptions, tags, publish times, lengths, public view counts) and top comments on those videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): views, engaged views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, likes, shares and subscribers gained or lost for the channel's videos.

The app cannot upload, edit, delete or publish anything, and does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce a weekly summary of how the channel's videos performed, which the channel owner uses to plan future videos. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. An AI assistant may read the summary on the channel owner's behalf to help draft the weekly plan.

Where it is stored and for how long

  • All data is stored as files on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server run by the app.
  • YouTube Analytics figures may be kept for as long as the app is in use, to track growth over time.
  • Other data (video titles, descriptions and comments) is refreshed weekly, and older copies are deleted after 30 days.
  • The sign-in token that lets the app read the data is stored in a local file and is never shared.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page, myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all stored data. Any deletion request sent to the contact below will be completed within 7 days.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
