Hint of History Analytics

A small private tool used by the owner of the Hint of History YouTube channel to read the channel's own performance statistics once a week.

It uses read-only access to view:

  • the channel's public videos and their titles,
  • YouTube Analytics for those videos (views, watch time, retention, subscribers gained),
  • top comments on the channel's own videos.

It cannot upload, edit or delete anything, and it is not offered to anyone else. The data stays on the channel owner's own computer and is used only to plan future videos.
